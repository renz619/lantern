WHAT TO IGNORE

- An item counts when it is resting on its own on furniture, on a computer, or on the floor, with no one touching it. A person sitting or standing nearby does not matter, as long as they are not touching the item.
- If you are unsure whether a person is wearing, holding, or touching an item, leave it out.

CHARGERS AND CABLES
- Chargers count even when they are plugged in. Check for charging cables, wall adapters, and power bricks that are plugged into a computer, a monitor, a power strip, or a wall outlet, as well as ones lying loose. Report each charger you find as "charger", and say what it is plugged into in "position" (for example "plugged into the PC").
- Never refer to a person in "position". Use furniture and landmarks only.

AIRPODS AND EARBUD CASES
- Airpods and earbuds are very often stored in a small case, and the case can be black, white, or any color, plain or with a ring, strap, or lanyard attached. Do not require seeing the earbuds themselves.

KEYS

PENS AND PENCILS
- Use "pencil" for pencils and for mechanical pencils. If you cannot tell whether a slim writing tool is a pen or a pencil, use "pencil".

WALLETS AND CARD HOLDERS

HOW TO REPORT EACH ITEM
Return one entry for every separate physical item. If the room has two wallets or three water bottles in different places, report each one separately. Do not report the same physical item twice if it is just seen again later in the video; report it once, at the moment it is clearest.

Fields:
